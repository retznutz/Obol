# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Nuxt (Vue), chosen by the owner. Packaging and shell (browser kiosk, Electron, Tauri) are undecided.

## Users

Emulation hobbyists who download Obol and install it on their own setups, usually a PC or mini PC connected to a TV. The people who then use it day to day are the hobbyist plus their household and guests on the couch, many of whom never set the system up and should not need to.

## Product Purpose

Obol is the screen between turning the machine on and playing a game: it presents the library by system, lets a person pick a game and launch it, and gets out of the way. Success means anyone in the room can pick up the input in their hand and be playing within a few presses, and the owner is proud to show the collection.

## Positioning

Two commitments together, where most frontends pick one: **couch simplicity** (no one has to touch settings to play) and **collection pride** (the library is presented with care: art, metadata, history and curation).

## Operating Context

- A TV viewed from the couch, in a living room, often dim.
- Navigation is spatial focus movement; there is no reliance on a pointer.
- Sessions are short trips through the UI on the way to a game, plus longer browsing when someone is showing off the collection.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Inputs (required):** keyboard and gamepad. Every screen must be fully operable with either. On-screen prompts show keys or pad buttons, depending on which device was used last.
- Mouse and touch are not required inputs.
- **Terminology:** "system" for an emulated console or computer; "game" for a title in the library; "core" for an emulator back end.
- **Open decisions (not yet confirmed):**
  - which emulators or cores Obol launches, and how;
  - where metadata and art come from (scraping source);
  - target operating systems;
  - licensing and distribution;
  - how ROMs and BIOS files are supplied (assumed to come from the user, not confirmed).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Obol**.
- Logo: oxblood wordmark with a sun striped in five bands. Master PNG and derived variants live in the Obol design system: https://claude.ai/artifact/AocEDPg3p7ZFXPrdoEqBcX
- Voice: short, plain, verb first; sentence case; no exclamation marks or emoji; errors say what broke, then what to do.

## Evidence on Hand

- The logo PNG (1432 x 632, transparent) and the design system above.
- No screenshots, users, testimonials, download numbers or press exist yet. Do not fabricate them.
- No licensed box art or game library is on hand. Use clearly fictional titles and placeholder art in mocks, and never present them as real games.

## Product Principles

1. **Anyone can play.** A guest with no setup knowledge reaches a game without help or a settings screen.
2. **The library is the hero.** Presentation serves the collection; chrome steps back.
3. **Every action names its input.** What each key or button does is always visible on screen.
4. **Problems explain themselves.** Missing files or broken setups are stated plainly, with the fix, at the point where they block play.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Full keyboard and gamepad operability on every screen; nothing depends on hover or pointer precision.
